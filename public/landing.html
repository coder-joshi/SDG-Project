About the project

Why a shared registry matters

India's Green Credit Programme, run by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, encourages voluntary environmental actions. Those actions need records that every party can check.

The problem

  • Claims are hard to check without site visits and paperwork.
  • The same action can be counted twice across registries.
  • Buyers cannot easily trace where a credit came from.

What GreenLedger does

  • Stores every action, review and sale as a permanent ledger record.
  • Requires accredited verifiers to approve a claim before credits exist.
  • Lets buyers see the full history of each credit they hold.
How it works

From action to certified credit in three steps

  1. Act and register

    A seller logs an action such as plantation, water conservation or recycling, and uploads geo-tagged photos and documents.

  2. Verify and certify

    An accredited verifier reviews the evidence, approves or rejects the claim, and the decision is recorded for audit.

  3. Trade and retire

    Approved credits are listed on the marketplace. Buyers purchase them and retire them against compliance or CSR goals.

Who it's for

One platform, three roles

Sign in to a role based account. Each role gets its own dashboard and permissions.

Seller

Register projects, submit evidence and list verified credits for sale.

Verifier

Inspect submitted projects and approve or reject claims with a recorded reason.

Buyer

Browse certified credits, purchase them and track the impact you retire.
